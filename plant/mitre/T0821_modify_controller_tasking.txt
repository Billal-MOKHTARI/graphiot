MITRE ATT&CK for ICS — Technique T0821: Modify Controller Tasking
Tactic: Impair Process Control

Description.
Adversaries may modify the tasking of a controller — the sequence, timing or selection of
the operations it carries out — so that it acts on the process differently from the
operator's plan. This includes changing which recipe or operating mode a stage follows, or
the ordering and timing of steps, so the controller executes a valid program at the wrong
moment in the overall sequence.

Relevance to this cell.
Each stage follows a work order and a loaded recipe and advances through phases (idle, ramp,
steady, transition). Modifying controller tasking here means changing the effective tasking
of a stage through the gateway — loading or applying the wrong recipe/phase settings for the
current work order, or forcing a stage into a phase's operating point ahead of the process
being ready for it — so the stage runs a legitimate operation out of sequence.
