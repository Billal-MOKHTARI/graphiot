MITRE ATT&CK for ICS — Technique T0831: Manipulation of Control
Tactic: Impact

Description.
Adversaries may manipulate control of the physical process to cause it to behave outside
the operator's intent while the control system continues to appear to function. Rather than
disabling equipment, the adversary steers the process through ordinary control actions so
that product or equipment is degraded. Because each action is a normal control operation,
the manipulation is visible only in the process outcome, which may lag the actions.

Relevance to this cell.
Control of mixing, coating+drying and slitting is exercised by writing setpoints and
commands through the gateway. Manipulation of control here means driving a process output
(viscosity, dryer temperature, web tension, coating mass loading, residual solvent, edge
quality) away from where it should be for the active work order, using only writes the
system accepts, so the effect appears downstream in product quality.
